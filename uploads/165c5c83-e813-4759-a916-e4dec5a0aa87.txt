Sandcastle and Building

Tae-Wan Kim

Sand is piled up on the ground.
A foundation is driven deep into the ground.

A sandcastle without a foundation rises quickly.
A building progresses more slowly,
because it takes time to lay its foundation beneath the ground.

The sandcastle reaches toward the sky before we know it,
while the building rises slowly,
layer by layer.

The building is completed.
The sandcastle keeps adding more sand,
trying to rise higher than the building.

At that very moment,
the sandcastle suddenly collapses with a roar
and leans against the building.
