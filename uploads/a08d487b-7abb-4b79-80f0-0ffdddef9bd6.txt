Waiting

Kim Tae-wan

When will it come closer?

One glass of makgeolli.

When will it come closer?

Two glasses of makgeolli.

Will it ever come closer?

Three glasses of makgeolli.

Still no sign.

Four glasses of makgeolli.

No promise.
No answer.

Another glass of makgeolli...

The waiting drifts farther away,
while the makgeolli fills the glass.

And when it finally came closer,
it had already become a dream.
